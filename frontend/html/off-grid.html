Grid connection choices

Energy independence
has a price.

Understand the practical difference between grid-tied, hybrid and off-grid solar before sizing a system.

⌁

Grid-tied

Panels reduce daytime grid purchases. Most standard grid-tied inverters shut down during outages unless designed with approved backup capability.

  • Usually lowest system complexity
  • Grid still supplies shortfalls
  • Export value depends on local rules
◐

Hybrid

Combines solar, grid and battery storage. Can support selected loads during outages when correctly configured.

  • Flexible backup and self-consumption
  • Battery adds cost and maintenance
  • Grid connection usually retained
☀

Off-grid

Designed to operate without utility supply. Requires storage and enough generation for low-sun periods, often with contingency generation.

  • Highest resilience potential
  • Seasonal sizing risk and higher cost
  • Careful load management needed

Don't pay for independence you don't need

Staying connected can be financially preferable where the grid is available and the extra storage needed for winter resilience costs more than expected savings. Compare actual tariffs, outage needs and full equipment quotes using the system comparison.
