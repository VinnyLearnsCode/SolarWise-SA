Compare the details,
not just the price.

Record up to three proposals using the same checklist. This worksheet does not rank installers or verify their claims.

Before comparing

  • Ask each installer to quote the same capacity, backup loads and expected annual generation.
  • Compare the exact panel, inverter and battery models, warranty terms and exclusions.
  • Ask for an itemized price, expected maintenance, replacement assumptions and grid-connection costs.
  • Verify certifications, references, insurance, municipal requirements and every claim independently.

Do not enter account numbers, identity numbers, payment details or other sensitive information. No quote files are uploaded.

Changes save automatically on this device.

Use your own judgment

The worksheet summarizes information you enter; it cannot check installer competence, product authenticity, legal compliance, system design or whether a quote is good value. Ask a qualified independent professional if anything is unclear.
