    Three different capacities

    Battery (kWh) is stored energy. Inverter (kW) is the power it can deliver at one time. Solar array (kWp) is panel nameplate power. Starting surges, temperature, battery limits and wiring affect real designs. Have a qualified installer verify these figures.
