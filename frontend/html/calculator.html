Before requesting quotes

A practical next-step checklist

  • Use 6–12 months of electricity bills to confirm kWh, tariff blocks and fixed charges.
  • Ask for an on-site roof, orientation, shading and structural assessment.
  • Request itemized equipment, installation, warranty, maintenance and replacement costs.
  • Compare at least two proposals using the same usage, tariff and system assumptions.
  • Confirm municipal registration, grid-connection and export requirements with your provider.

How to read this

A useful estimate, not a promise.

Generation is modeled from system capacity and solar irradiation, adjusted for system performance losses. Bill savings are limited by your usage and the share of solar energy used in your home. Annual maintenance and tariff escalation are included in long-term estimates. Results are nominal, undiscounted rand values; battery dispatch, tariff blocks and export income are not modeled.
