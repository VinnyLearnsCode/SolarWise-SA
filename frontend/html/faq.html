Search common solar questions. Answers explain general concepts and do not replace a site-specific quote or current official guidance.

Will solar work on cloudy days?

Panels still generate diffuse light electricity, but output is lower than in strong direct sun. The amount depends on cloud thickness, equipment and location. Batteries can shift energy but do not create it.

How much will my electricity bill fall?

It depends on your actual tariff, when you use electricity, solar size, shading, system losses, battery dispatch and fixed charges. Use the calculator as an estimate and compare against a detailed installer design.

Does a grid-tied solar system work during a power outage?

Many standard grid-tied inverters disconnect during outages for safety. Backup requires appropriately designed equipment and circuits, and may need a battery.

Does every municipality pay for exported solar electricity?

No. Tariffs, eligibility, export compensation and connection rules vary. Check current published rules directly with your municipality and do not assume export income.

Is off-grid always the best choice?

No. Off-grid resilience may require more panels, batteries and contingency supply for winter or extended low-sun periods. Remaining connected can cost less where a reliable grid is available.

Are your solar and weather figures live readings?

No. PVGIS provides modeled solar potential; Open-Meteo provides weather observations and forecasts. Results are labeled by source and retrieval date and are not readings from your roof.
