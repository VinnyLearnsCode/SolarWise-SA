About SolarWise SA

Better energy decisions,
without the sales pitch.

SolarWise SA helps households explore whether solar may make practical and financial sense for their property.

Independent by design

The platform is designed to make assumptions visible, distinguish modeled data from live readings, and give users tools without requiring an account. It is not an installer marketplace and does not guarantee a particular system output or saving.

South African electricity tariffs and municipal connection requirements vary. The most useful assessment starts with your bill and a verified local tariff.
