SolarWise works without optional analytics. This notice explains the analytics choice available on this site.

Optional analytics and consent

Google Tag Manager (container GTM-PQRTDNDG) is not loaded unless you choose “Accept analytics.” If accepted, tags configured in that container may collect page or interaction information, device/browser information, and online identifiers such as cookies, and send it to Google. The exact data and retention depend on the tags, Google Analytics property, and settings configured by the site operator.

Declining optional analytics does not affect calculators, saved scenarios, or other planning tools. The consent choice is stored in this browser’s local storage; it records only the choice and its update time. No account is required.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using Privacy settings at the bottom of any page. Google tags in the container must be configured to respect Google Consent Mode and require analytics consent. Advertising storage and advertising-related consent remain denied by the site.

Other information you enter

Calculator inputs and saved assessments are processed in the browser or sent to the SolarWise calculation API when you request a calculation. Saved assessments and quote worksheets are kept in browser storage. Do not enter account numbers or other sensitive identifiers. Feedback submitted through the contact form is sent to and stored by the SolarWise service.

Third-party services and South African privacy

If you accept analytics, Google’s services are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. The site operator should configure the Google Analytics property, data retention and deletion settings, provide contact details, and assess applicable obligations under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
