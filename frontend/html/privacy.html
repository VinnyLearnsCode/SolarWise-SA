This notice explains what information SolarWise handles and how to contact the site operator.

Who operates this site

This site is operated by Thabiso Monaheng. For privacy questions or requests, contact abutvincent23@gmail.com.

Analytics

Google Tag Manager and other optional analytics are currently disabled. The site does not load the Google Tag Manager container. Analytics will remain disabled until its tags and data practices have been reviewed.

Other information you enter

Calculator inputs and saved assessments are processed in the browser or sent to the SolarWise calculation API when you request a calculation. Saved assessments and quote worksheets are kept in browser storage. Do not enter account numbers or other sensitive identifiers. Feedback submitted through the contact form is sent to and stored by the SolarWise service.

If you request location-based solar or weather information, the selected coordinates are sent to the SolarWise API and used to request modeled solar data from PVGIS or forecast data from Open-Meteo. When PostgreSQL is configured, weather snapshots are cached with coordinates rounded to approximately 100 metres for up to 7 days to reduce repeat requests; expired records are checked for deletion daily. The solar explorer loads its charting library from jsDelivr.

Retention and privacy requests

Feedback messages and any email address included with them are kept for 365 days from submission, then deleted from the live database. Expired feedback is checked for deletion daily, so a record may remain for up to one additional day; a service or database outage may delay cleanup until the next successful run. Privacy-request correspondence is kept for 365 days after the request is closed, then deleted from the operator's mailbox where controllable. Information may be kept longer only where reasonably necessary to resolve a request, address a security incident, or meet a legal obligation. Hosting-provider backups may retain deleted database records until those backups expire under the provider's separate schedule.

To make an access, correction, deletion, objection or complaint request, email abutvincent23@gmail.com. Include the email address used when submitting feedback, if any, and enough non-sensitive detail to identify the relevant submission. Do not send account numbers, identity-document copies or other sensitive information unless the operator specifically requests what is necessary to verify the request. The operator will acknowledge requests within 5 business days and aims to provide a substantive response within 30 calendar days. If more information or time is needed, the operator will explain why and provide an expected response date. Browser-local scenarios can be deleted in the browser or through the site's saved-plan and quote controls; exported files are under the user's control.

South African privacy

The site operator should assess applicable obligations under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). This notice is informational and is not legal advice.
